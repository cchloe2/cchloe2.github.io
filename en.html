Hi, I'm Chloé

I grew up in Tahiti, where I took my baccalaureate at Lycée Paul Gauguin, before crossing the world to study business computing in Bordeaux. Today I live and work in Vendée, on the Atlantic coast of France.

My job is to make data useful. I model, transform and shape a company's numbers so that business teams know where they stand, and what to decide.

Since 2014 I have had a second workshop: I create websites, logos and videos for freelancers and companies in France and French Polynesia. That is where I learned that a dashboard is also a piece of design.

I work in agile teams, close to the business, with one fixed idea: an indicator should make sense in five seconds.
